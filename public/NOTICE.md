# Notices for third-party data, fonts and models

The sky data under `tree-of-life-sphere/` is generated by `scripts/gen-sky.ts` from
third-party sources. It is **not** covered by the repository's MIT license;
each part keeps the license of the work it comes from.

## `art/*.webp` — constellation figures

Illustrations by Johan Meuris, from the "Modern" sky culture of
[Stellarium](https://stellarium.org). Converted from PNG to greyscale WebP;
otherwise unchanged.

Licensed under the [Free Art License](https://artlibre.org/licence/lal/en/).
You may copy, distribute and modify them, provided you credit the author and
keep them, and anything derived from them, under the same license.

## `sky.json` — figure anchors (the `art` entries)

Which three stars pin each figure to the sky, and where in the image, are
taken from the same sky culture's `index.json`, © Stellarium's team,
[CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/). The star
positions have been converted to the sphere's own coordinates.

## `sky.json` — stars, constellation lines and names

From [d3-celestial](https://github.com/ofrohn/d3-celestial), © 2015 Olaf
Frohn, BSD 3-Clause License. Positions have been converted to the sphere's
own coordinates (ecliptic, with longitude counted from Regulus as 0° Leo).

## `/fonts/Astronomicon.ttf` — astrological glyphs

Astronomicon by Roberto Corona (https://astronomicon.co), used for the
planet and sign glyphs on the Vault page. Licensed under the SIL Open Font
License 1.1; the licence text is beside the font as `Astronomicon-OFL.txt`.

## `/models/body/*.glb` — human figures

The realistic female and male bodies from Blender Studio's Human Base
Meshes bundle (v1.4.1), released by the Blender Foundation under
[CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/) (public
domain). How they were exported is in `models/body/README.md`.

## `/draco/` — Draco decoder

Google's [Draco](https://github.com/google/draco) mesh decoder, copied
unchanged from three.js, used to load the compressed body models. Licensed
under the Apache License 2.0.
